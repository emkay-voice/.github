<h1 align="center">Emkay</h1>

<p align="center"><b>A stock <a href="https://openvoiceos.org">OpenVoiceOS</a> voice assistant, running in your browser.</b><br>
Phone, tablet, TV or a Raspberry Pi in kiosk mode – nothing to install.</p>

<p align="center"><a href="https://app.emkay.ai/"><b>Open Emkay</b></a> · <a href="https://emkay.ai/">emkay.ai</a> · <a href="https://github.com/emkay-voice/emkay">Source</a></p>

---

Emkay runs OpenVoiceOS – core, skills, the GUI with the Mycroft Mark II face and the OVOS control
panel – inside a web page with Pyodide. The browser is the hardware: microphone, speaker,
location, sensors and camera, each answering OVOS the way the PHAL plugin of a device does.

"Emkay" is "MK" said out loud, a nod to Mycroft's Mark I and Mark II.

**Status: early alpha.** Twelve languages, speech through OpenVoiceOS' public servers, skills
installable from the control panel. On-device speech, a wake word and HiveMind are next.
